About TASG

Operator-led and combat-experienced.

Terminal Applied Solutions Group is an independent defense innovation company focused on turning consequential field problems into disciplined concepts, protected intellectual property, and partner-ready development pathways.

Oklahoma-foundedEstablished 2026Independent private company

Company purpose

Start where the operational problem is still unclear.

TASG works at the front end of capability development: clarifying the user problem, shaping the concept, preserving the invention record, and preparing a responsible path toward engineering, validation, manufacturing, and transition.

01

Identify

Define the operational problem and why existing approaches leave a meaningful gap.

02

Formulate

Translate that gap into requirements, concept logic, technical questions, and protected records.

03

Transition

Connect the concept to organizations capable of engineering, testing, funding, manufacturing, and scaling it.

Founder

Nick Sulewsky

Founder, Terminal Applied Solutions Group

Nick brings more than 15 years of military leadership experience spanning Army fires, joint effects, small-team operations, operational planning, advising, instruction, and organizational leadership.

His background includes deployed service in Afghanistan and Iraq, support to special operations formations, security-force assistance activities in Central and South America, and the training and development of military leaders. Those experiences shaped his focus on practical capability gaps that directly affect people operating at the tactical edge.

He founded TASG to translate operationally significant problems into disciplined capability concepts, early prototypes, and protected intellectual property—then work with qualified defense-industry partners that can provide the engineering depth, testing infrastructure, manufacturing capacity, and market access needed to mature them.

Nick holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and is pursuing a Master of Business Administration.
