About TASG

Operator-led. Independent. Product-first.

Terminal Applied Solutions Group develops disciplined capability concepts and partner-ready pathways for the tactical edge. Current focus: Terminal Link employment reporting into TAK, with selective advisory and a SNARE-1A co-development study.

Oklahoma-founded Established 2026 Independent private company

Company purpose

Start where the operational problem is still unclear.

TASG works at the front end of capability development: clarifying the user problem, shaping the concept, preserving a careful invention record, and preparing a responsible path toward engineering, validation, manufacturing, and transition.

01

Identify

Define the operational problem and why existing approaches leave a meaningful gap.

02

Formulate

Translate that gap into requirements, concept logic, technical questions, and careful records.

03

Transition

Connect the concept to organizations capable of engineering, testing, funding, manufacturing, and scaling it.

What we offer publicly

Lead product, selective advisory, co-development study.

TL

Terminal Link

Lead product. Captures an operator-initiated employment event and reports who, where, and when to TAK while keeping initiation local.

Founder

Nick Sulewsky

Founder, Terminal Applied Solutions Group

LinkedIn

Nick brings more than 15 years of military leadership experience spanning Army fires, joint effects, small-team operations, operational planning, advising, instruction, and organizational leadership.

His background includes deployed service in Afghanistan and Iraq, support to special operations formations, security-force assistance activities in Central and South America, and the training and development of military leaders. Those experiences, attributed to the founder personally, shaped TASG’s focus on practical capability gaps at the tactical edge.

He founded TASG to translate operationally significant problems into disciplined capability concepts and partner-ready pathways, then work with qualified organizations that can provide engineering depth, testing infrastructure, manufacturing capacity, and market access.

Nick holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and is pursuing a Master of Business Administration.
