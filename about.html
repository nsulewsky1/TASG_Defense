About TASG

Operator insight. Industrial intent.

TASG exists to shorten the distance between a consequential field problem and a credible capability pathway. The company is intentionally small by design and serious by standard.

Oklahoma-foundedEstablished 2026Combat veteran-owned

Operating model

Small enough to move. Disciplined enough to matter.

TASG does not need to become a vertically integrated prime to create value. Its role is to develop the front end of the capability pathway and then align with organizations built to take the next step.

01 / Observe

Identify recurring operational problems.

Start with repeated end-user pain points and real-world mission context rather than technology looking for a use case.

02 / Define

Clarify role, constraints, and evidence needs.

Shape the concept around the intended user, operating environment, burden, desired effect, and the questions that must be answered next.

03 / Architect

Build a partner-ready concept path.

Create documentation, requirements framing, development boundaries, and a transition story that engineers and decision-makers can evaluate seriously.

04 / Transition

Work toward the right downstream owner.

Concepts are developed with engineering, manufacturability, testing, industrialization, and acquisition in mind from the beginning.

TASG

Founder perspective

Built from problems seen firsthand.

Nick Sulewsky founded Terminal Applied Solutions Group, LLC to convert recurring warfighter problems into disciplined capability concepts and transition opportunities.

His background includes Army fires and joint-effects leadership, deployed operations, advising, instruction, and small-team mission support. That experience informs TASG’s focus on user burden, practical employment, realistic development gates, and transition-minded concept framing.

TASG operates independently. The company and its concepts are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Department of Defense, the U.S. Army, or any current or former unit.

Company standards

Credibility before attention.

TASG uses the public website to communicate serious intent, not exaggerated claims.

Accuracy

Say what is true now.

Stages, risks, and partner needs should be communicated honestly without overstated readiness or invented test data.

Discipline

Protect what creates value.

Technical architecture, invention records, calculations, and controlled materials remain outside the public website and repository.

Transition

Design for the next owner.

Concepts should be framed so an engineer, manufacturer, acquirer, or strategic investor can understand the path forward.

Operator-led defense innovation focused on disciplined concept development, survivability-oriented capability exploration, and transition to the right engineering and industrial partners.
