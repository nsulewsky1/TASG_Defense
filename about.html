Operator insight. Industrial intent.

Operating model

Small by design. Serious by standard.

TASG does not need to become a vertically integrated prime to create value. It develops the front end of the capability pathway and aligns with organizations built to finish it.

NSFounder / Operator-led innovation

Founder

Built from problems seen firsthand.

Nicholas Sulewsky founded Terminal Applied Solutions Group, LLC to convert recurring warfighter problems into disciplined capability concepts and transition opportunities.

His background includes more than a decade of Army fires and joint-effects leadership, deployed operations, security-force advising, instruction, and small-team mission support. That experience informs TASG's emphasis on user burden, practical employment, integration with existing systems, and honest development gates.

TASG operates independently. The company and its concepts are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Department of Defense, the U.S. Army, or any current or former unit.

Company standards

Credibility before attention.

01 / Accuracy

Say what is true now.

Development stages, evidence, risks, and partner needs should be communicated honestly without fictional readiness or performance claims.

02 / Discipline

Protect what creates value.

Technical architecture, invention records, calculations, drawings, and controlled partner materials remain outside the public website and repository.

03 / Transition

Design for the next owner.

Concepts are developed with engineering, manufacturability, testing, acquisition, integration, and industrial scale in mind.
