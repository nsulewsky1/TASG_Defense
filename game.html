Interactive training demo

SNARE Drone Intercept Simulator.

A browser-based concept demo in which you defend friendly positions against tethered FPV quadcopter threats using a net-launcher intercept mechanic. Difficulty increases over time and the top 10 scores are stored on the device.

Desktop and mobile friendlyTop-10 local leaderboardProgressive difficulty

Controls: move your mouse or finger to aim, click or tap the game area to fire, or press Space on desktop. Press P to pause. Stop drones before they drop ordnance or slam into the position.

How to play

Intercept hostile drones before they reach the position.

  • FPV kamikaze drones dive straight for the defended position.
  • Grenadier drones try to drop explosives from overhead.
  • Your projectile expands into a net on impact or after a short flight.
  • Every wave gets faster and denser. Survive as long as possible.
