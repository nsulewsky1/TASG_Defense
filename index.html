What TASG does

Turn operational gaps into credible development paths.

TASG is an operator-led defense innovation company. We concentrate on the earliest and often neglected part of capability development: defining the real problem, shaping a disciplined concept, and preparing it to move through the right technical and industrial team.

01 / Identify

Start with the mission.

Find repeated, consequential problems through operator experience, field research, and practical employment analysis.

02 / Architect

Shape the solution.

Translate the gap into requirements, employment logic, interfaces, risk questions, documentation, and an evidence plan.

03 / Transition

Build the right team.

Align the concept with engineering, manufacturing, funding, testing, and acquisition partners positioned to make it real.
