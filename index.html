Lead product

Terminal Link

C-sUAS employment reporting for TAK

Built for handheld and pouch-carried final-layer effectors. An employment signal is checked locally, then published as structured TAK reporting: who employed, where, and when. No remote arm or fire.

Designed to queue the event locally when transmission is disabled and publish when policy permits.

The gap

One employment. One shared event.

Effectors and position reporting already exist. What is often missing is a coherent employment event on the common picture. Terminal Link reports the employment. Initiation stays local.
