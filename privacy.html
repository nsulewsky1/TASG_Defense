Company information

Privacy.

Effective July 2026

This page describes the information practices of the public website operated by Terminal Applied Solutions Group, LLC at tasgdefense.com. It is intended as a clear operational notice, not as legal advice.

Information collected by the website

The current static website does not contain advertising trackers, marketing pixels, user accounts, payment processing, or a server-side contact database operated by TASG. Basic technical logs may still be generated by the hosting, domain, or content-delivery provider as part of operating and securing the site.

Inquiry form

The partner inquiry form runs in the visitor's browser and prepares a message in the visitor's default email application. The form does not transmit the entered information to a TASG web server. Information is sent only when the visitor chooses to send the resulting email.

Email communications

When a visitor emails TASG, the information in the message is received and retained through the applicable email service to respond, assess an engagement, maintain business records, and protect legal or security interests. Do not send classified, export-controlled, procurement-sensitive, proprietary, or operationally sensitive information in an initial public inquiry.

Cookies and analytics

TASG does not currently place its own analytics or advertising cookies through this website. Hosting providers may use essential technical mechanisms for security, routing, or performance. This notice should be updated before adding analytics, embedded video, third-party forms, or other services that change these practices.

External services

Email applications, domain registrars, hosting providers, and other services used to access or communicate with TASG operate under their own privacy terms. TASG does not control those independent services.

Contact
