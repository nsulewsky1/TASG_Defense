Current capability focus

SNARE-1A public profile.

A compact, soldier-carried counter-sUAS concept intended to address the final close-range survivability gap for dismounted personnel.

Stage: concept maturationNext gate: funded prototypeStatus: not fielded or tested

Operational role

Close the final gap.

SNARE-1A is being shaped around a narrow operational problem, not a broad claim to replace layered counter-sUAS defenses.

01

Threat context

Close-proximity small-UAS threats can compress detection, decision, and response time beyond the practical reach of conventional defensive options.

02

Intended user

Dismounted personnel who require a rapidly accessible last-layer option that does not depend on an external network or dedicated crew.

03

System role

A complementary final layer intended for situations in which earlier detection, electronic, kinetic, or maneuver options are unavailable, exhausted, or too slow.

04

Transition model

TASG defines and protects the concept, then works with qualified partners to complete engineering, testing, qualification, integration, and production.

Design priorities

Public principles, not technical instructions.

01 / Access

Rapidly available

Employment must fit the compressed timelines associated with a close-proximity threat.

02 / Burden

Soldier-centered

Size, weight, carry position, controls, and training demand must respect the existing combat load.

03 / Dependence

Non-RF-dependent

The concept is intended to retain utility when electronic conditions, interference, or threat architecture reduce the value of RF-only approaches.

04 / Scale

Production-minded

Development decisions should support a credible path to repeatable manufacture and broad distribution.

05 / Integration

Layer-compatible

The system should complement—not compete with—the detection, command-and-control, electronic, and kinetic layers around it.

06 / Evidence

Test-driven

Claims should follow defined measures, engineering analysis, prototype results, and controlled evaluation—not precede them.
