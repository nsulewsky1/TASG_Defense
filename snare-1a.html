Public capability profile

SNARE-1A.

A soldier-carried, close-range counter-sUAS concept focused on final-layer survivability. This page communicates the operational problem, design intent, and development posture without disclosing protected technical details.

Current status: Concept maturationPublic posture: Mission-level disclosureNext gate: Funded prototype

Why it matters

Close-proximity drone threats compress decision time.

Small-UAS threats can arrive too quickly, too close, or under conditions that limit the usefulness of traditional defensive layers. That creates a final-layer survivability gap for dismounted personnel.

Operational role

Final defensive layer

SNARE-1A is aimed at the last moments of survivability when speed, proximity, and user burden matter more than broad-area solutions.

Design intent

Compact and rapidly accessible

The concept is being shaped around quick access, intuitive use, and compatibility with realistic soldier load and movement constraints.

Transition posture

Built for the next partner

Documentation and concept framing are oriented toward engineering, prototype, test, manufacturing, and acquisition conversations.
